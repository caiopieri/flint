Why Flint

Three apps. None of them whole.

You handwrite in GoodNotes, but it can't link a thought to anything. You connect ideas in Obsidian, but its handwriting is an afterthought. You let AI help in Notion, but your notes live on someone else's servers. Flint is the one place that does all three — and keeps it yours.

Built on principles you own

Local-first

Works fully offline. No account, no cloud required, nothing phones home by default.

Plain text, forever

AI, on your terms

An assistant that lives in your notes — not in the cloud.

Search everything you've ever written, summarize, and turn scattered captures into real notes. Run a small model on-device, or plug in your own API key — the choice, and your data, stay with you.
